## How to read this

- A verdict needs 10+ days with both a passer and a failer. The range resamples days in blocks of 5; helps or hurts means the whole range is on one side of zero.
- Rows before 10/21 are re-scored with today's thresholds and count only when the weak- and strong-stock profiles agree; later rows use the decision the report logged.
- This compares names, not causes: passers and failers differ on the other rules too. A verdict is a reason to review a rule, not to change it.
